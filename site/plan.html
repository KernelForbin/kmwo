The plan

8 weeks, Nov 16 to Jan 10. Three sessions a week: Lift, Ride, Lift.

Lift

About 20 to 25 minutes. Building gym, or home with light weights.

  1. Goblet squat
  2. Dumbbell bench press
  3. One-arm dumbbell row (each side)
Weeks 1–2
2 sets of 10
Weeks 3–8
3 sets of 10

Rest about a minute between sets. When every set of 10 feels easy, go up one weight next time.

Ride

Peloton bike. Pick any class that fits.

Weeks 1–2
20 min, low impact
Weeks 3–8
30 min, low impact or endurance
  • From week 5, one ride a week can be a harder class (HIIT, Tabata, Power Zone) if you feel like it. Optional.
  • Take the post-ride stretch.

Rules

Default slot: 8:15 pm. After bedtime. Same time every session.

Missed a day? Do the next session on your next free day. Don’t double up and don’t make it up.

Short on time? 10 minutes at home counts: 2 rounds of 10 goblet squats, 10 push-ups, 10 rows per side.

Holidays. Dec 25 and Jan 1 fall on session days. The 10-minute home version counts, or skip and pick up the sequence after.

After week 8. If three a week feels automatic, add a fourth session (a Lift). Don’t make sessions longer.
